Products by Kal’s Digital

Two products, built from patterns we kept seeing.

Some problems are unique to one business, and they call for a tailored engagement. Others repeat across many businesses. We turned two of those into products, each with its own purpose, audience and way of working.

Product one

KARD

Make every introduction count.

KARD connects a physical introduction to a digital experience. A professionally printed card carries a QR code that opens a landing page built for your business, and a professional email address on your own domain completes the picture.

For
Business owners, professionals, brands and organizations, and restaurants that want guests to open the menu on their phones.
Comes as
The standard card-and-page KARD, tailored KARD websites, and the restaurant QR menu.
Price
KARD Pack ₦170,000: 100 printed cards, a custom landing page and a professional email. Websites and QR menus are quoted on scope.

Product two

slotùSlotù

Show your work. Fill your calendar.

Slotù gives appointment-based professionals one organised home for their portfolio, their services and a clear way to book. Clients request a time and pay a deposit by bank transfer, and you manage every booking from a private owner desk.

For
Makeup artists, barbers, hairstylists, lash and nail technicians, massage therapists, dentists and other appointment-based professionals.
Comes as
A branded portfolio and booking website. Pricing and project start are on the Slotù website.

The Slotù website, getslotu.vercel.app.

Which one fits

Start with how your customers find you.

KARD and Slotù solve different problems. Some businesses need one, some need both, and some need neither.

If this sounds like you Start with
You meet people in person, at events, in meetings or across a counter, and want that moment to lead somewhere. KARD
You want a website that carries your own identity, with your work, your services and a clear way to reach you. KARD websites
You run a restaurant or venue and want guests to open your menu on their own phones. KARD restaurant QR menu
You sell appointments, your work is visual, and bookings currently happen one message at a time. Slotù
None of these quite fits, or several things need to change at once. A tailored engagement
