The problem

Your work is on social media. Your bookings are in your messages.

Social media is good at showing work and poor at taking bookings. Clients scroll to find your prices, message to ask which days are free, and wait for a reply.

A Slotù website brings your portfolio, your services and a clear way to book into one organised home that belongs to you. Clients can see the quality of your work, understand what you offer, and take the next step without waiting for you to answer.

How a Slotù booking works

From first look to confirmed slot.

Clients pay a deposit the way they already pay: by bank transfer. You stay in control of every confirmation.

  1. 01

    See the work

    Your portfolio, grouped by category, in photos and video.

  2. 02

    Choose a service and time

    Every service with its price and duration. Only free times are offered, and a time can’t be booked twice.

  3. 03

    Pay the deposit

    The client pays into your bank account, uploads the receipt and gets a booking code to check their status.

  4. 04

    You confirm

    Once you have seen the payment in your account, you confirm the booking in your owner desk.

Card payments and automatic confirmation are not part of Slotù today. You confirm each booking yourself after checking the deposit.

Your private owner desk

Every booking in one place.

Behind your public site sits a private desk, protected by sign-in, where you run the day-to-day.

  • Confirm or decline bookings

    See each request with its service, time and the receipt the client uploaded.

  • Hold times fairly

    Unpaid bookings release their time automatically after the hold period you choose.

  • Change your own details

    Update your services, prices and opening hours yourself, without waiting for us.

  • Separate logins

    You and your team each sign in with your own account.

  • Booking emails

    An email to you for each new booking, and a reminder before confirmed appointments, set up and tested with a real email before we say it works.

  • Checked before launch

    Your site goes through automated checks for speed, accessibility, links and the booking flow before you see it.

Why Slotù

Not a generic website with a booking button added.

Slotù’s booking system comes from the one we built for GLAM BY OYIN, a makeup and lash artist whose booking website is live at glambyoyin-bookings.vercel.app.

  • Built around booking

    Services, prices, durations, open times and deposits are designed into the site from the start, not bolted on later.

  • Your identity, not a template

    Your colours, type and logo throughout, so the site looks like your business and nobody else’s.

  • Made for how clients pay

    Deposits by bank transfer with receipt upload, which suits clients who already pay professionals that way.
