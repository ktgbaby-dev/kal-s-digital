The problem

Most introductions end at a phone number.

Someone takes your card, puts it in a pocket and is left to do the rest: remember who you are, search for you, and guess which profile is current.

Meanwhile your work, your services and your best explanation of what you do sit somewhere they will never see. KARD closes that gap. The card does what a card does well, which is to make a good first impression. The page carries the detail: who you are, what you offer and how to get in touch.

One idea, adapted to how you meet people.

The standard KARD connects your business card to a page. KARD websites take the same thinking further. The restaurant QR menu brings it to the table.

Questions

Before you start.

How much does a KARD cost?

The KARD Pack is ₦170,000. It includes 100 professionally printed cards, a custom landing page and a professional email address on your own domain, subject to domain and email setup. KARD websites and restaurant QR menus are priced on their scope, and we send you a quotation before any work starts.

Do I need my own domain for the email?

Yes. A professional email address sits on a domain that belongs to you, so its setup depends on that domain. We talk through the options with you when we scope the project.

Is KARD the same as Slotù?

No. KARD connects an introduction to a digital presence. Slotù is a portfolio and booking website for appointment-based professionals. Some businesses need one, some need both.

Can the QR menu take orders or payments?

Not as standard. The QR menu presents your dishes, descriptions and prices, and guests order the way they already do. If you need more, we scope it with you and confirm it in writing before work begins.

Can I change what my page says later?

Yes. Tell us what has changed and we will agree how updates are handled for your KARD when we set it up.
