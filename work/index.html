Our work

Work that carries each client’s own identity.

A beauty artist, a fashion label, an identity studio, a hand-dye clothing brand and a music artist. Five businesses with five different problems, and five websites that look nothing alike. Every one was designed and built by Kal’s Digital, and every one is live.

01 · Makeup, lashes and beauty

GLAM BY OYIN

A makeup and lash artist whose brand is built on one line: “Beauty, taken seriously.” We built her brand website, then a booking website where her clients choose a service and pay a deposit.

glambyoyin.vercel.app

Scroll inside the frame to see more

What we built

  • A brand website with the artist’s philosophy, films of her makeup and lash work, a portfolio in two collections and her story
  • A separate booking website: services with prices and durations, open times, and deposits by bank transfer with receipt upload
  • Booking codes clients use to check their booking, and a private owner desk with separate logins
  • Email alerts for new bookings and reminders before appointments

Visual direction

The client’s own identity: Bodoni Moda and Jost, onyx and glam pink, and her Wingline pattern.

  • #0E0B0D
  • #E0147C
  • #F2A7C3
  • #FBF6F3

Why it matters

The booking system we built here became the foundation of Slotù, our portfolio and booking product.

02 · Fashion and lifestyle

ENLUX

A fashion and lifestyle label of bold, minimal pieces for people who were never made to blend in. Its line is “Pain made me timeless”, and its site is built as a manifesto rather than a shop window.

enlux.vercel.app

Scroll inside the frame to see more

What we built

  • A single-page brand site told in chapters: the brand, its philosophy, the collection, a manifesto and its codes
  • A collection section for pieces and accessories, with enquiries handled directly by the brand
  • Contact by WhatsApp and email throughout
  • Logo files prepared from the supplied artwork for use on the web

Visual direction

Black, white and light only. Cormorant for statements, an expanded Archivo for labels, and a script reserved for two words.

  • #050506
  • #F6F5F2
  • #C4C6CB

03 · Creative identity studio

Forge Visuelle

An identity studio for businesses, founders and creatives, positioned as “a creative identity architect, not a logo shop”. The site sets out that thinking alongside the services.

forge-visuelle.vercel.app

Scroll inside the frame to see more

What we built

  • A three-page site: home, about and contact
  • Services set out as brand strategy, visual identity, and naming and positioning, with the audiences each one serves
  • An about page telling the founder’s story and philosophy
  • An enquiry form that opens the visitor’s email app with the message ready to send

Visual direction

Warm darks and amber on an architectural grid, set in Playfair Display and Inter.

04 · Hand-dyed clothing

Vistosa The Brand

A label that turns plain clothing into one-of-a-kind pieces by hand-dyeing them. No two pieces are alike, so the site explains the process and invites commissions.

vistosa-pi.vercel.app

Scroll inside the frame to see more

What we built

  • A three-page site: home, about and contact
  • The process in four steps: choose the piece, design from scratch, dye by hand, set and finish
  • A lookbook of recent pieces
  • A commission route by email, phone or Instagram, with an enquiry form

Visual direction

Black and white, so the dyed colour of each piece leads. Allura script with Jost.

  • #000000
  • #FFFFFF

05 · Music artist

KTG MUSIC

“Not an artist. A channel.” The site presents a music artist as a television channel, with programming rather than a discography.

ktgmusic.site

Scroll inside the frame to see more

What we built

  • A single page organised as a broadcast: On Air, The Library, Programming, Meet the Character, Stay Tuned and Transmit
  • Streaming tracks embedded from Spotify, and performance videos
  • A fan sign-up form
  • Served on its own domain, ktgmusic.site

Visual direction

Broadcast colour bars on cream and black, with Anton, Permanent Marker and Space Grotesk.
