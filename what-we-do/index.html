What we do

Practical technology, chosen for the problem.

Every engagement starts with a business problem, then draws on the capabilities that solve it. Most clients need two or three of the services below, rarely all of them. We scope what your business actually requires, and nothing more.

01

Digital presence

The problem we see: customers can’t find you online, or don’t trust what they find. The website is out of date, the profiles are half finished, and search results point somewhere else.

What changes: a professional presence that explains what you offer and makes the next step obvious.

  • Website design and development

    Single-page brand sites and multi-page websites, designed around your offer and your identity, and built to load quickly on phones.

  • Business landing pages

    Focused pages for a service, a launch or a campaign, each with one clear next step.

  • Search engine optimization

    Sound site structure, page titles, descriptions and content that help search engines understand what you do and who it is for.

  • Social media setup and optimization

    Complete, consistent profiles that match the rest of your brand, with content planning and ongoing management where we agree it.

02

Brand and communication

The problem we see: your business looks different everywhere it appears. The logo changes from flyer to profile, the colours drift, and nobody is sure what the brand should sound like.

What changes: one coherent identity across logo, colour, type, graphics and content, so people recognise you wherever they meet you.

  • Brand strategy

    What you stand for, who you serve and how you say it, written down so every touchpoint agrees.

  • Visual identity

    Logos, colour palettes, type and brand patterns, delivered as organised files with guidance on how to use them.

  • Graphic design and visual communication

    Social posts, flyers, brochures, menus and business cards that look like they come from the same business.

  • Content creation and creative campaigns

    Graphics and short videos produced or coordinated to your brief, and campaign concepts that explain an offer quickly.

03

Digital systems

The problem we see: touchpoints that don’t connect, and manual steps that slow customers down. Enquiries arrive in five places. Bookings are confirmed one message at a time.

What changes: fewer dead ends for your customers, and less repetitive work for you and your team.

  • Digital customer experiences

    QR experiences, enquiry routes and booking journeys that take a customer from interest to action without a detour.

  • Workflow automation

    Practical automation for repetitive steps, scoped after we have seen how the work is done today.

  • Business process improvement

    Mapping how work and information move through your business, then removing the steps that slow it down.

  • Custom digital solutions

    When the problem is unusual, we design something specific to it, and tell you plainly what it will and won’t do.

How a project runs

Diagnose. Design. Build. Refine.

The exact steps depend on what you choose. These four stages hold for every project.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We learn how your business works, who your customers are and what you want to improve. If the answer is not what you expected, we say so.

    From you: a conversation, and access to what already exists.

  2. 02

    Design

    We recommend a solution and confirm what will be built, and what will not. You approve the specification before any build work starts.

    From you: logos, colours, services, contact details and your approval.

  3. 03

    Build

    We design and build around the approved specification, and connect it to the touchpoints you already use.

    From you: quick answers when we need a detail.

  4. 04

    Refine

    We test what we built, including forms, emails and links, review it with you, make agreed changes and hand over what you need to run it.

    From you: a careful review and a final sign-off.

Clarity

Scope, inclusions and dependencies are agreed up front, so nobody is surprised later.

Collaboration

Approvals and information need your participation. We make every request specific.

Testing

Features such as email delivery and forms are tested before handover, not assumed.

Timelines and any ongoing support are agreed for each project before work begins.
